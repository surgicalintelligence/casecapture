Before you start
  • Never enter patient identifiers: no names, MRNs, dates of birth, phone numbers or room numbers, in any field. CaseCapture removes the identifiers it recognizes, but it cannot catch everything.
  • You are responsible for this data. Keep this device passcode-locked, and send or store exported CSV files only where your institution's policies allow.
  • Codes are suggestions. Final codes must match your operative note and payer rules. CaseCapture is not billing, coding or legal advice.
  • Back up regularly. Your device can clear this app's storage. Export your CSV often; it is your backup.
